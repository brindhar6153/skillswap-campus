Time Bank Mechanics

Knowledge is the Only Currency

No money changes hands on SkillSwap Campus. The system operates on a direct, audited credit ledger system:

1. Earn Credits

Tutor a fellow student in a skill you excel in. For every completed hour of teaching, you earn 1.0 time credit.

2. Spend Credits

Spend your earned credits to learn from other campus mentors. A 1-hour learning session costs 1.0 credit.

3. Direct and Flex Swaps

Swap directly with a peer (e.g. French for Calculus) or accumulate credits from one user and spend them on someone else.
