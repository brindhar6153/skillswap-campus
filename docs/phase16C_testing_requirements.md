# Google Play Testing Requirements & Release Workflow Guide

This document clarifies testing tracks, pre-release verification, and Google Play policy requirements for **SkillSwap Campus**.

---

## 1. Testing Policy Status: Personal vs. Organization Accounts

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Testing Requirements Depend on Account Status:**
> `DEPENDS ON GOOGLE PLAY ACCOUNT STATUS`
> 
> * **Personal Developer Accounts (Created after November 13, 2023)**:
>   Google Play requires that you run a **Closed Test with at least 20 testers opted-in continuously for at least 14 consecutive days** before you are eligible to request access to the Production track.
> * **Personal Developer Accounts (Created before Nov 13, 2023) or Organization Accounts**:
>   Production track access is available immediately without the mandatory 20-tester/14-day opt-in requirement (though internal testing is still strongly recommended).

---

## 2. Testing Track Overview

Google Play provides three distinct pre-release testing tracks in the Play Console:

| Testing Track | Maximum Testers | Google Review Required? | Recommended Usage for SkillSwap Campus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Internal Testing** | Up to 100 testers | **No (Available within minutes)** | First step: Upload `dist/SkillSwapCampus-final.aab` and test with your own personal device to verify Play Store installation. |
| **Closed Testing (Alpha)** | Unlimited (by email list) | **Yes (Fast automated/policy check)** | Second step: Invite campus peers / student testers. If using a newer personal account, run for 14+ days. |
| **Open Testing (Beta)** | Unlimited (public URL) | **Yes** | Optional public beta testing. |
| **Production** | All Play Store users | **Yes (Full Policy Review)** | Final public release. |

---

## 3. How to Set Up Closed Testing (If Required by Account Status)

1. Navigate to **Testing → Closed testing** in the Play Console.
2. Click **Create track** and name it `Campus Alpha Testers`.
3. Under **Testers**, create an email list (or Google Group) containing the Gmail addresses of your testers.
4. Upload `dist/SkillSwapCampus-final.aab` to this track.
5. Provide the **Opt-in link** (generated by Google Play) to your testers.
6. Testers click the link on their Android devices, tap **"Become a tester"**, and download the app directly from the Google Play Store.
7. Maintain active opt-in for at least 14 consecutive days before clicking **Apply for production**.

---

## 4. Pre-Production Release Verification Checklist

Before promoting any build from testing to production, verify:
* [ ] App installs cleanly from Google Play without signature conflicts.
* [ ] Student registration with `.edu` email succeeds.
* [ ] Backend API responds over HTTPS without network timeouts.
* [ ] Skills portfolio saves and updates properly.
* [ ] Reciprocal match explorer displays peer matches.
* [ ] Swap requests and session scheduling function end-to-end.
* [ ] Time-bank credit ledger increments and decrements accurately.
* [ ] Push/in-app notifications deliver alerts.

---

## 5. Gathering & Recording Tester Feedback

* Testers can submit private feedback directly through Google Play by opening the app's store listing and tapping **"Developer feedback"**.
* View feedback in Play Console under **Quality → User feedback → Private feedback**.
