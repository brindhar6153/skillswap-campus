# Google Play Content Rating Questionnaire Guide: SkillSwap Campus

This guide provides the exact answers required for the **Content rating** (IARC) questionnaire in the Google Play Console for **SkillSwap Campus**, based strictly on the current application features.

---

## 1. Category Selection
* **Category**: **Utility, Productivity, Communication, or Other** (or **Education**)
* **Email Address for Rating Notices**: Your developer contact email.

---

## 2. Questionnaire Responses

### A. Violence
* **Does the app contain violence against humans, animals, or fictional characters?**  
  👉 **No**

### B. Sexuality & Nudity
* **Does the app contain any sexual content, nudity, or depiction of sexual themes?**  
  👉 **No**

### C. Language & Profanity
* **Does the app contain offensive language, crude humor, or profanity?**  
  👉 **No** (The app provides academic peer exchange; standard user conduct rules apply).

### D. Controlled Substances
* **Does the app reference or encourage alcohol, tobacco, drugs, or gambling?**  
  👉 **No**

### E. User Interactions & Sharing
* **Does the app natively allow users to interact or exchange content with other users through voice, text, or sharing images?**  
  👉 **Yes** (Students can send swap requests with custom invitation text and submit post-session feedback comments).
* **Does the app share the user's current and precise physical location with other users?**  
  👉 **No** (Only user-typed meeting venue text like "Campus Library" is entered manually by users; no GPS tracking is used).
* **Does the app allow users to purchase digital goods with real money?**  
  👉 **No** (The app operates strictly on an internal, non-monetary time-bank credit economy; no real-money in-app purchases or subscriptions exist).

---

## 3. Expected Rating Certificate Results

Based on these answers, the IARC rating authority will assign the following ratings:

| Rating Authority | Expected Rating | Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ESRB (North America)** | **Everyone (E)** / **Teen (T)** | Parental guidance/social interaction disclaimer due to user-generated messages |
| **PEGI (Europe)** | **PEGI 3** / **PEGI 12** | Social interaction feature |
| **USK (Germany)** | **USK 0** / **USK 6** | Educational utility |
| **ClassInd (Brazil)** | **L (General Audience)** | Free for all ages |
| **IARC Generic** | **Everyone** | Users Interact |

---

## 4. Target Audience & Content
* **Target Age Groups**: Select **18 and over** (University & College students).
* **Appeal to Children**: Select **No** (This app is not designed to appeal primarily to children under 13).
