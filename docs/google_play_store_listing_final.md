Welcome to SkillSwap Campus — the peer-to-peer student learning and skill exchange network built for university and college campuses.

Learn new skills, master coursework, and share your knowledge directly with fellow students through a cashless time-bank credit system.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🎓 WHAT IS SKILLSWAP CAMPUS?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Finding reliable study partners and tutoring on campus shouldn't be complicated or expensive. SkillSwap Campus connects students who want to learn with peers ready to teach.

Whether you want to learn Python programming, work through Calculus problem sets, practice conversational French, or build graphic design skills, SkillSwap Campus enables you to exchange knowledge 1-on-1 with other students without monetary fees.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
✨ CORE FEATURES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. 🏛️ VERIFIED CAMPUS COMMUNITY
• Register and log in using your institutional (.edu) university email address.
• Set up your academic profile with your major, graduation year, and course background.

2. 🧠 SKILLS PORTFOLIO
• Choose from foundational academic, programming, language, and design skill categories.
• Specify your proficiency level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced) for subjects you can teach and subjects you want to learn.

3. 🤝 COMPATIBILITY MATCHING
• Browse fellow students with compatible skill sets.
• Highlight reciprocal matches where you and a peer can teach each other complementary skills.

4. ⏱️ TIME-BANK CREDIT ECONOMY
• Receive 2.0 free welcome credits upon registration to get started immediately.
• Earn 1.0 credit for each hour you teach.
• Spend 1.0 credit for each hour you learn.
• Automatic credit holds during scheduled sessions ensure clarity and prevent double-booking.

5. 📅 SESSION SCHEDULING & SWAP REQUESTS
• Send exchange invitations with personalized notes proposing a skill swap.
• Accept or decline incoming proposals.
• Schedule 1-on-1 sessions with custom dates, durations, and campus meeting venues (e.g., campus library or study hall).

6. ⭐ DOUBLE-BLIND REVIEWS
• Submit post-session ratings (1 to 5 stars) and feedback comments.
• Ratings unlock simultaneously only after both participants submit their reviews, ensuring honest and fair feedback.

7. 🔔 IN-APP ALERTS
• Receive notifications for incoming swap requests, session confirmations, and unlocked review feedback.

1. Download and open SkillSwap Campus.
2. Register with your university (.edu) email.
3. Select your teaching skills and learning goals.
4. Discover matching campus peers, send a swap request, and schedule your tutoring session!

Empower your learning journey with SkillSwap Campus!
